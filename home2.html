Hands-On Studio Infrastructure

Equipped Like a Real Fashion Design Atelier

Great tailoring requires the right workspace. Our institute provides wide pattern-drafting tables, calibrated sewing machines, fitting mannequins, and embroidery cot frames so you learn with professional tools from Day 1.

Dedicated Machine Stations

Practice bobbin tension, presser-foot swaps, cord piping attachment, and concealed zipper stitching on smooth-running sewing machines.

Full-Scale Drafting Tables

Draft brown-paper slopers using L-scales, hip curves, and French curves before transferring chalk lines onto folded cloth.

Dressform Fitting Lab

Inspect armhole ease, bust dart contour, neckline symmetry, and sleeve hang on studio mannequins prior to final hemming.

Multi-Fabric Handling

Learn grainline control and lining stabilization across cotton, poplin, raw silk, Kanjeevaram zari borders, crêpe, and net.

Daily Classroom Flow

How Every Practical Studio Session Works

Each class is divided into three structured hands-on intervals so you build drafting accuracy and sewing speed together.

Part 1: Measurement & Paper Drafting

Record garment formulas in your pattern notebook and draft full-scale paper blocks checked by your trainer.

Part 2: Chalk Layout & Supervised Cutting

Align fabric grainlines, mark seam allowances with tailor's chalk, and cut main and lining panels cleanly.

Part 3: Machine Assembly & Fit Check

Stitch darts, necklines, sleeves, and piping on your assigned machine and verify fit on a studio dressform.
