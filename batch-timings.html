6. Flexible Learning Options

Designed Around Your Family & Work Commitments

We understand that homemakers, mothers, and students occasionally face unexpected family events or exam schedules. Our training structure focuses on milestone completion rather than rigid lecture attendance.

Missed-Session Makeup Support

If you miss a cutting or stitching day with prior notice, our trainers help you catch up on that exact garment step in your next session.

Self-Paced Practical Progression

Move to the next pattern block only after you feel confident with your current seam quality and fitting accuracy.

Batch Transfer Assistance

Need to switch from a morning slot to an afternoon or weekend slot due to schedule changes? Coordinate with our desk to transfer smoothly.

7. How to Choose Your Batch

Batch Selection & Machine Allocation Process

Follow these simple steps to confirm your training slot and reserve your sewing machine station.

01

Select Your Course & Time Window

Choose from our 8 specialized tailoring or embroidery courses and identify whether Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend suits your routine.

02

Verify Current Machine Seat Status

Because each student practices on an individual sewing machine or Aari cot frame, contact our admissions desk to verify real-time seat availability for your preferred slot.

03

Receive Your Starter Material Checklist

Once your batch date is confirmed, our trainers provide a simple checklist of drafting paper, measuring tape, thread spools, and cotton practice cloth for Day 1.
