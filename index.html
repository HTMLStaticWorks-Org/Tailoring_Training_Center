Step-by-Step Studio Process

Our 4-Stage Practical Training Methodology

We demystify tailoring into clear, repeatable stages so even a first-time learner develops accuracy without fear of cutting fabric.

01

Measure & Analyze

Learn how to take 14+ accurate body measurements, understand ease allowances, and note posture adjustments before touching the shears.

02

Draft on Paper First

Create full-scale brown-paper pattern drafts using simple mathematical formulas for armholes, neck depths, and dart points.

03

Fabric Layout & Cutting

Master fabric grainline alignment, economical cloth folding, chalk marking, and clean scissor handling on cotton, silk, and lining fabrics.

04

Stitch, Fit & Finish

Assemble the garment on the sewing machine, add piping or embroidery accents, verify fit on a dressform, and apply boutique-quality pressing.
