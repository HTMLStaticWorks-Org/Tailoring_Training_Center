Structured Progression

Beginner to Advanced Learning Path

Not sure where to start? Follow our four-step skill progression designed to build your confidence layer by layer.

Stage 1

Machine & Stitch Foundations

Master pedal control, bobbin tension, straight & curved seams, hand hemming, and basic fabric folds.

Basic Tailoring
Stage 2

Core Ethnic Garments

Draft and stitch everyday chudidhars, salwar bottoms, kurtis, and 4-dart plain & lining saree blouses.

Chudidhar & Blouse
Stage 3

Contoured Patterns & Cuts

Advance to princess cut, katori cut, collar necklines, concealed zippers, and custom size pattern blocks.

Pattern & Advanced
Stage 4

Bridal & Boutique Craft

Combine Aari embroidery, Zardosi work, and surface styling to create high-value festive and bridal wear.

Aari & Garment Making
Hands-On Advantage

Practical Training Benefits That Make the Difference

Every course at Velora is structured around real workbench practice so that you leave with garments you actually stitched yourself.

Individual Drafting Verification

Your paper pattern is checked line-by-line by a trainer before you cut into your cloth.

All Fabric Types Covered

Learn how to handle cotton, poplin, silk, crêpe, georgette, and net with proper lining.

Mistake-Proof Seam Formulas

Simple measurement charts for neck width, armhole depth, and dart length.

Take-Home Stitched Portfolio

Build a physical collection of sample seams, paper patterns, and finished garments.
